2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on a web server of an external hosting provider in Germany. When you open it, the server automatically stores your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, your browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website securely and to detect errors and attacks (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The log files are deleted after seven days at the latest, unless they are needed to clarify a security incident.

3. Surveys and votes

Surveys and votes are stored without an account and without your name. A survey consists of the title, description, category, end date, questions and answers you enter; please do not include personal data in them. A vote only stores the selected answers. The data is kept in a database of Supabase Inc. (USA), which processes it on my behalf under a data processing agreement. Supabase is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of data protection for transfers to the USA. To answer requests, Supabase also processes your IP address for a short time. Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, my interest in providing the survey tool. Surveys are deleted when the project is discontinued or on request.

4. Local storage

So that you cannot vote twice by mistake, your browser remembers the IDs of the surveys you took part in, in its local storage. This entry stays on your device and is not sent to the server. It is strictly necessary for the function you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

5. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising. The fonts are stored on this server, so no connection to external font services is made.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data erased or its processing restricted (Art. 17 and 18 GDPR),
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

Just write to the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner.
